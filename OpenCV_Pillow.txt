OpenCV (Open Source Computer Vision Library) and Pillow (Python Imaging Library or PIL) are two popular libraries in Python used for image processing.
Each has its strengths and weaknesses, and the choice between them depends on the specific needs of your project.

OpenCV: Best for: High-performance, real-time image processing, video analysis, and machine learning.
Strengths:
• Fast and optimized for computer vision tasks.
• Supports a wide range of image and video formats.
• Has many advanced image processing algorithms (e.g., face recognition, feature detection).
• Can work with both static images and live video streams.

Pillow (PIL): Best for: Simple image manipulation, basic image editing, and transformations.
Strengths:
• User-friendly and easy to integrate into applications.
• Great for basic image processing tasks like resizing, cropping, filtering, and format conversion.
• Works well with a variety of image formats.
